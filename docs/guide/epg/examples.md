# Template Examples

Looking for ready-made templates? Start with the shipped [starter set](../templates/defaults) — ten Gracenote-modeled templates seeded on every install, covering team and event channels across the major sports.

## Importing and Exporting

1. In Teamarr, go to **EPG → Templates** and click **Import**
2. Select a template JSON file

Any template can also be **exported** from its row actions on the Templates page — the natural way to share your own or move templates between installs.

## Contributing Templates

Have a template you'd like to share? Join the Dispatcharr Discord and share it in the Teamarr channel.
